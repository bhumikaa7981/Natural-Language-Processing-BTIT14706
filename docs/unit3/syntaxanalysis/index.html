You know that one movie moment where the villain drops the mic with a line so clean the whole theater goes silent? That line didn't just sound right — it was built right, word slotting into word like a grammar rule clicking into place. That's syntax analysis: is this sentence legal, and if so, how did it get built? Below, you get to be the parser. Every step is generated live by an actual algorithm running in your browser — not a gif, not a screenshot. Click through slowly, type your own sentences (or grammars), and watch exactly where it says yes, where it says no, and why.
