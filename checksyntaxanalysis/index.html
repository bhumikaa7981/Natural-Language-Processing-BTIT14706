A sentence is basically a cricket team — everyone needs a role, a position, and to actually follow the rules.

Before a computer can understand what you typed, it has to do three things in order: figure out what each word is, figure out how those words fit together, and check that everything actually agrees. That's syntax analysis. Below are three live, click-through tools — one for each step.

🏏 Cricket-team version: POS tagging decides who's a batsman and who's a bowler. Parsing works out the batting order and field placements. Constraint-based grammar double-checks the playing XI actually follows the rules before the umpire lets play begin.

🎯 Why syntax analysis matters

Computers don't have a "gut feeling" for grammar. Every rule your brain applies instantly has to be made completely explicit before a machine can use it. Skip this stage, and every downstream task inherits the confusion.

🌍 Where you've already met it

Google Docs underlining "the dogs runs" in red — that's constraint checking. Siri figuring out "book" is a verb vs a noun — that's POS tagging. Every code editor's autocomplete — that's leaning on parse structure.
